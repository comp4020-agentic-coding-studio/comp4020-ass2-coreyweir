Each week takes one approach seriously: what it is, what it buys, and the limit
it runs into. The limit is usually what the following week exists to answer.

Several concerns recur rather than being finished — processes, the filesystem,
the network, performance — and they deliberately return in new contexts. Week 5
gives a program a filesystem; week 7 is about what happens when two runtimes
need the same one. That is the same subject twice, which is the point.

Lectures are short and link out. The detail lives on the pages that own it.
