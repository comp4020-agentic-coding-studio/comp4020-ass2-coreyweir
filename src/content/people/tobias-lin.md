Tobias is writing a thesis on ahead-of-time translation of machine code to
WebAssembly, which makes him the only person here qualified to say what is
genuinely new.

His contribution is largely deflationary and entirely useful. Bring him an idea
you are pleased with and there is a fair chance he will produce a paper from
2011 that did it first, usually on hardware a thousand times slower. This is not
discouragement. Knowing the prior art is how you work out which part of the
problem is actually yours.

He tutors the sessions and takes weeks 9 and 10, on the grounds that he is the
only one who enjoys them.
