Nadia teaches alongside Corey and oversees the sessions.

She spent a decade keeping other people's distributed systems alive, which left
her with firm views about the word "just". She is the reason this course marks
trade-off reasoning rather than working demonstrations — she has seen a great
many working demonstrations.

She will not tell you whether your approach is right. She will ask what it would
cost you to be wrong about it, which is more useful and considerably more
annoying.
